How the grade was built

    Pillars, latest year

    Bars show the oriented z-score: right of the line is sounder than the typical bank, left is weaker.

    Ratio detail, latest year

    Asset quality and earnings ratios are scored on the weaker of the latest value and the three-year average. Values outside the calibration's 1st to 99th percentile are capped. Capital ratios add at most z = +1.
